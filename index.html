Common Questions

Is this a drop-off only service?

Yes. We operate exclusively as a drop-off service to ensure focus on quality craftsmanship and efficient scheduling. This allows us to dedicate our full attention to protecting your vehicle properly.

Is a deposit required to book?

Yes. A $50 deposit is required to secure your appointment slot. This ensures we reserve dedicated time for your vehicle's care.

What does paint protection film cover?

Our paint protection film safeguards high-impact zones including door handles, door edges, rear bumper loading edge, and rear fender sections—areas most prone to chips, scratches, and environmental damage.
